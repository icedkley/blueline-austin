Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for the service call?

No. The service call and diagnostic are always free for homes. You get a written price before any work starts, with no obligation.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, when the schedule has room. We're open Monday–Friday 7:30 AM–6 PM and Saturday 8 AM–2 PM. Call (512) 555-0147 or book online for the soonest time. After hours, our automated booking assistant answers and schedules you for the next opening.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Financing is available for repairs and new systems through our partner GoodLeap, so you don't have to pay it all at once. Apply online with GoodLeap or ask your technician. Subject to credit approval.

What should I do if I smell gas or my CO alarm goes off?

Get everyone out of the house right away. Don't flip light switches, use a lighter, or start a car in the garage. Once you're outside and at a safe distance, call 911, and for a gas smell also call Texas Gas Service at 800-700-2443. After the utility says it's safe, call us and we'll repair the furnace, water heater, or gas appliance. Every heater check we do includes a carbon monoxide (CO) test.
